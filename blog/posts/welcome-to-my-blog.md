I replaced the hidden notes flow with a normal blog page so writing is easier to maintain and publish.

From now on, posts live under `blog/posts/` and are listed in `blog/posts/index.json`.

Publishing a post is just:

1. Write a markdown file.
2. Add metadata in `index.json`.
3. Set `"draft": false`.
4. Commit and push.
